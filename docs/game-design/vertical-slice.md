# Vertical slice scope

Maddox Takashi is a betrayed futuristic samurai rebuilt by mechanic Daichi Morimoto. His revenge against Kurogane Corporation conflicts with an experimental cybernetic arm's violent impulses and his refusal to kill surrendered enemies.

The current playground verifies movement, camera, jumping, sprinting, simple interaction, and reusable health. It deliberately contains no narrative branching or combat. The next focused encounter should explore the surrender rule with a single enemy and a clearly observable consequence, before introducing progression or larger regions.

Longer-term ideas (multiple characters, combat specializations, skill trees, cinematic transitions, four interconnected regions) remain design goals rather than architectural requirements for this milestone.
